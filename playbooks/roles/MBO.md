# Mission/Business Owner (MBO) — Operational Playbook

> Network-agnostic role playbook. Applies regardless of network architecture, classification enclave, or technology stack. Source: [JSIG §1.5.11](../../reference/JSIG-source/section-1.5-roles-and-responsibilities.md), cross-referenced to [MAINTENANCE-PLAN.md](../../MAINTENANCE-PLAN.md) and the [JSIG control-families INDEX](../../reference/JSIG/control-families/INDEX.md).

## 1. Role Summary

The MBO has operational responsibility for the mission or business process supported by the mission/business segment or information system, and is the key stakeholder for system lifecycle decisions (e.g., agency/component head, chief executive officer, chief financial officer). The MBO ensures information security requirements are integrated into the system development life cycle process, and must be a U.S. citizen and government employee ([JSIG §1.5.11](../../reference/JSIG-source/section-1.5-roles-and-responsibilities.md)).

## 2. JSIG-Defined Responsibilities (verbatim, JSIG §1.5.11)

- Has operational responsibility for the mission or business process supported by the mission/business segment or the information system.
- Is the key stakeholder for system lifecycle decisions (e.g., agency/component head, chief executive officer, chief financial officer).
- Ensures that information security requirements are integrated into the system development life cycle process.
- Must be a U.S. citizen and a government employee.

## 4. Coordination Interfaces

- **Information System Owner (ISO):** the ISO addresses the operational interests of the user community that the MBO's mission/business process serves; the MBO provides mission requirements the ISO must satisfy ([JSIG §1.5.11](../../reference/JSIG-source/section-1.5-roles-and-responsibilities.md), [§1.5.12](../../reference/JSIG-source/section-1.5-roles-and-responsibilities.md)).
- **Authorizing Official (AO):** the MBO is a key stakeholder whose mission/business risk tolerance factors into the AO's authorization decision ([JSIG §1.5.5](../../reference/JSIG-source/section-1.5-roles-and-responsibilities.md)).
- **Information Owner/Steward:** coordinates on information handling requirements that affect the mission/business process ([JSIG §1.5.10](../../reference/JSIG-source/section-1.5-roles-and-responsibilities.md)).
- **Risk Executive (Function) (REF):** the REF ensures authorization decisions consider all factors necessary for mission and business success, which draws directly on MBO input ([JSIG §1.5.2(e)](../../reference/JSIG-source/section-1.5-roles-and-responsibilities.md)).

## 5. Key Artifacts Owned/Produced

- Mission/business requirements documentation feeding the SDLC and SSP.
- Lifecycle decision records for the mission/business system (modernization, sustainment, decommissioning).
- Business-continuity priorities/inputs to the contingency plan.
- Resourcing/budget justifications for mission-supporting IS security.
