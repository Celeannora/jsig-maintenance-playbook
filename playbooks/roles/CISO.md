# Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) — Operational Playbook

> Network-agnostic role playbook. Applies regardless of network architecture, classification enclave, or technology stack. Source: [JSIG §1.5.4](../../references/JSIG-source/section-1.5-roles-and-responsibilities.md), cross-referenced to [MAINTENANCE-PLAN.md](../../MAINTENANCE-PLAN.md) and the [JSIG control-families INDEX](../../reference/JSIG/control-families/INDEX.md).

## 1. Role Summary

The CISO, also known as a Senior Information Security Officer (SISO), carries out the CIO's security responsibilities under FISMA and serves as the primary liaison between the CIO and the organization's AOs, Information System Owners (ISOs), Common Control Providers (CCPs), and ISSM/ISSOs. The CISO must be a U.S. citizen and government employee, must possess professional qualifications for the role, must treat information security as a primary duty (not collateral), and heads an office resourced to help the organization achieve more secure information and systems ([JSIG §1.5.4](../../references/JSIG-source/section-1.5-roles-and-responsibilities.md)).

## 2. JSIG-Defined Responsibilities (verbatim, JSIG §1.5.4)

The CISO shall:
- Possess professional qualifications, including training and experience, required to administer the information security program functions;
- Maintain information security duties as a primary responsibility; and
- Head an office with the mission and resources to assist the organization in achieving more secure information and IS, in accordance with the guidance provided by their respective CA for FISMA compliance. All FISMA activities shall be maintained within channels and reported to the CA SAPCO.

The CISO may also be appointed with the mission and resources to coordinate, develop, implement, and maintain an organization-wide information security program. [PM-2]

## 4. Coordination Interfaces

- **CIO:** carries out the CIO's FISMA security responsibilities; reports program status and effectiveness data feeding the CIO's annual report ([JSIG §1.5.3](../../references/JSIG-source/section-1.5-roles-and-responsibilities.md), [§1.5.4](../../references/JSIG-source/section-1.5-roles-and-responsibilities.md)).
- **Authorizing Officials (AOs):** primary liaison point for AO security-program coordination ([JSIG §1.5.4](../../references/JSIG-source/section-1.5-roles-and-responsibilities.md)).
- **Information System Owners (ISOs):** primary liaison for ISO-level security program matters ([JSIG §1.5.4](../../references/JSIG-source/section-1.5-roles-and-responsibilities.md)).
- **Common Control Providers (CCPs):** primary liaison for common-control program matters ([JSIG §1.5.4](../../references/JSIG-source/section-1.5-roles-and-responsibilities.md)).
- **ISSM/ISSO:** primary liaison channel between these operational security roles and the CIO ([JSIG §1.5.4](../../references/JSIG-source/section-1.5-roles-and-responsibilities.md)).
- **CA SAPCO:** all FISMA activities are maintained within channels and reported to the cognizant authority's SAPCO ([JSIG §1.5.4](../../references/JSIG-source/section-1.5-roles-and-responsibilities.md)).

## 5. Key Artifacts Owned/Produced

- Organization-wide information security program plan and implementation records (if appointed under PM-2).
- FISMA compliance activity reports to the CA SAPCO.
- Consolidated security posture reporting relayed from AO/ISO/CCP/ISSM/ISSO channels to the CIO.
- Evidence of CISO professional qualifications/training currency.
