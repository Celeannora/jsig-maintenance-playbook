**IR-1 INCIDENT RESPONSE POLICY AND PROCEDURES**

**Control:**  
The organization develops, documents, and disseminates to [Assignment: organization-defined personnel or roles]:

a. An incident response policy that addresses purpose, scope, roles, responsibilities, management commitment, coordination among organizational entities, and compliance; and

b. Procedures to facilitate the implementation of the incident response policy and associated incident response controls.

**Supplemental Guidance:**  
Incident response policy and procedures address the controls in the IR family that are implemented within the system and its environment of operation. The incident response policy can be included as part of the general information security policy for the organization. Incident response procedures can be developed for the security program in general, and for particular information systems, platforms, or applications when needed.

**IR-2 INCIDENT RESPONSE TRAINING**

**Control:**  
The organization provides incident response training to system users consistent with assigned roles and responsibilities:

a. Within [Assignment: organization-defined time period] of assuming an incident response role or responsibility; and

b. When required by system changes.

**Supplemental Guidance:**  
Incident response training is consistent with the assigned roles and responsibilities specified in the incident response policy and procedures. Incident response training includes use of incident response handling tools, techniques, and technologies, as appropriate. The organization updates incident response training [Assignment: organization-defined frequency] to reflect changes in policies, procedures, and practices; new techniques, technologies, and threats; and lessons learned.

**IR-3 INCIDENT RESPONSE TESTING**

**Control:**  
The organization tests the incident response capability for the system [Assignment: organization-defined frequency] using [Assignment: organization-defined tests].

**Supplemental Guidance:**  
Incident response tests include tabletop exercises, simulations, and other mechanisms that demonstrate the incident response capability of the system and the organization by exercising incident response procedures and capabilities. The organization may conduct a limited test to determine whether the incident response capability is operationally ready. Incident response testing is consistent with the frequency and complexity of the organization’s incident response capability and with the system and information being processed, stored, or transmitted by the system.

**IR-4 INCIDENT HANDLING**

**Control:**  
The organization implements an incident handling capability for incidents that is consistent with the incident response plan and the requirements of IR-1.

**Supplemental Guidance:**  
Incident handling includes preparation, detection and analysis, containment, eradication, and recovery. Incident handling also includes automated mechanisms to support incident handling. Depending on the nature and severity of the incident, incident handling may include activation of the organization’s incident response team, collection of evidence, protection of evidence, and analysis of the incident. The organization can use the provisions of the incident response plan to respond to incidents, including the assignment of roles, responsibilities, and actions to be taken.

**Related Controls:**  
AU-6, AU-8, AU-9, AU-11, IR-1, IR-2, IR-3, IR-5, IR-6, IR-7, IR-8, IR-9, IR-10, MP-6, SI-4, SI-7, SI-8

**IR-5 INCIDENT MONITORING**

**Control:**  
The organization tracks and documents incidents.

**Supplemental Guidance:**  
Incident monitoring is done to identify the need for response and determine the effectiveness of incident response activities. Monitoring may include the use of automated tools, event correlation, and trending to identify potential incidents, suspicious activity, and the need for additional response actions. Incident monitoring data may be used to support incident handling, reporting, and improvement of the incident response capability.

**IR-6 INCIDENT REPORTING**

**Control:**  
The organization requires personnel to report suspected incidents to the organizational incident response capability within [Assignment: organization-defined time period].

**Supplemental Guidance:**  
Incident reporting includes reporting of incidents to appropriate organizational officials and, when required, external organizations such as law enforcement, oversight authorities, and other stakeholders. The organization can establish criteria for what constitutes an incident, who must report, how to report, and where to report. The organization can also establish categories of incidents to support reporting and response prioritization.

**IR-7 INCIDENT RESPONSE ASSISTANCE**

**Control:**  
The organization provides an incident response support resource, integral to the organizational incident response capability, that offers advice and assistance to users of the system for the handling and reporting of incidents.

**Supplemental Guidance:**  
Incident response support resources can include a help desk, incident response team, or other designated resources. Support can be provided by personnel or automated means. The support resource may be used to provide guidance to users on identifying, reporting, and responding to incidents.

**IR-8 INCIDENT RESPONSE PLAN**

**Control:**  
The organization:

a. Develops an incident response plan that:

1. Provides the organization with a roadmap for implementing its incident response capability;

2. Describes the structure and organization of the incident response capability;

3. Provides a high-level approach for how the incident response capability fits into the overall organization;

4. Meets the unique requirements of the organization, including mission, size, structure, and functions;

5. Defines reportable incidents;

6. Provides metrics for measuring the incident response capability; and

7. Is reviewed and approved by [Assignment: organization-defined personnel or roles];

b. Distributes copies of the incident response plan to [Assignment: organization-defined personnel or roles];

c. Reviews the incident response plan [Assignment: organization-defined frequency];

d. Updates the incident response plan to address changes to the organization, mission, functions, or environment of operation and problems identified during plan implementation, execution, or testing; and

e. Protects the incident response plan from unauthorized disclosure and modification.

**Supplemental Guidance:**  
The incident response plan can be a single plan or a set of plans that address incident response for the organization as a whole, specific missions/business functions, specific systems, or specific types of incidents. The plan is consistent with the organization’s risk management strategy, complements related security plans, and includes the elements identified in the control.

**IR-9 INFORMATION SPILLAGE RESPONSE**

**Control:**  
The organization responds to information spills by:

a. Identifying the source and the affected systems and/or data;

b. Containing the spill;

c. Eradicating the spill;

d. Recovering from the spill;

e. Reporting the spill to [Assignment: organization-defined personnel or roles]; and

f. Implementing lessons learned from spills.

**Supplemental Guidance:**  
Information spills include instances where information is introduced into a system or media at an unauthorized security category or classification level. The response to a spill may include sanitization, reclassification, re-marking, replacement of media, notification, and other actions, as appropriate. Spill response actions are consistent with incident response procedures and applicable laws, regulations, policies, directives, and standards.

**IR-10 INTEGRATED INFORMATION SECURITY ANALYSIS TEAM**

**Control:**  
The organization establishes an integrated team of forensic, incident response, network, audit, and security personnel to achieve an effective incident response capability for the system.

**Supplemental Guidance:**  
The integrated team supports coordination among functions involved in incident response and analysis. Team members may be assigned from multiple organizational elements or external providers. The organization may include representatives from legal, human resources, public affairs, and other relevant functions as needed.
