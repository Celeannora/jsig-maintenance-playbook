| PE-1 | **PE-1 PHYSICAL AND ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION POLICY AND PROCEDURES** |
| Control | The organization develops, disseminates, and reviews/updates [Assignment: organization-defined frequency]: |
|  | a. A physical and environmental protection policy that addresses purpose, scope, roles, responsibilities, management commitment, coordination among organizational entities, and compliance; and |
|  | b. Procedures to facilitate the implementation of the physical and environmental protection policy and associated physical and environmental protection controls. |
| Supplemental Guidance | Physical and environmental protection policy and procedures address all controls in the PE family and other controls where physical and environmental protection controls are applied or have supporting applications. The policy can be included as part of the general information security policy for the organization. Physical and environmental protection procedures can be developed for the security program in general and for a particular information system when required. |

| PE-2 | **PE-2 PHYSICAL ACCESS AUTHORIZATIONS** |
| Control | The organization: |
|  | a. Develops, approves, and maintains a list of individuals with authorized access to the facility where the information system resides; |
|  | b. Issues authorization credentials for facility access; |
|  | c. Reviews the access list detailing authorized facility access by individuals and removes from the list individuals no longer requiring access; and |
|  | d. Notifies designated organizational personnel of individuals who have been removed from the access list. |
| Supplemental Guidance | Physical access authorizations apply to employees and visitors. Approval for unescorted physical access to facilities where information systems reside is strictly controlled and based on need-to-enter, competence, and trustworthiness. |

| PE-3 | **PE-3 PHYSICAL ACCESS CONTROL** |
| Control | The organization controls physical access to the facility and information system components within the facility and encrypts and protects physical access control devices [Assignment: organization-defined physical control devices] unless the device is a type that supports physical access control as a system component. |
| Supplemental Guidance | Physical access control applies to employees and visitors and is to be enforced at all facilities where information systems are housed. The organization uses physical access control systems and personnel security programs to protect facilities and information systems. The organization may use locks, guards, alarms, or other physical controls. |
|  | The organization protects and controls physical access control devices such as keys, access cards, locks, and biometric devices. Physical access control devices that are system components are managed in accordance with the system configuration management process. |

| PE-4 | **PE-4 ACCESS CONTROL FOR TRANSMISSION MEDIUM** |
| Control | The organization controls physical access to [Assignment: organization-defined transmission medium], which may include, but is not limited to, network cables, fiber-optic cable, coaxial cable, and other transmission media. |
| Supplemental Guidance | Physical access to transmission media is controlled to prevent unauthorized interception, damage, or modification. The organization protects transmission media both within and outside the facility. |

| Control | The organization controls physical access to information system output devices to prevent unauthorized individuals from obtaining the output. |
| Supplemental Guidance | The organization controls access to output devices based on the sensitivity of the information being output and the environment in which output devices are located. Output devices include monitors, printers, copiers, facsimile machines, audio output devices, and other devices that produce hardcopy or electronic output. |

| Control | The organization monitors physical access to the facility where the information system resides to detect and respond to physical security incidents. |
| Supplemental Guidance | Physical access monitoring includes the use of surveillance cameras, intrusion detection systems, guards, or other means to observe and record physical access and detect unauthorized access attempts. Monitoring is performed in accordance with applicable privacy and labor laws, directives, policies, and regulations. |

| PE-19 | **PE-19 INFORMATION LEAKAGE** |
| Control | The organization protects against or limits the effects of information leakage from the information system to the physical environment by: |
|  | a. Employing [Assignment: organization-defined measures] to control information leakage from the information system into surrounding areas; and |
|  | b. Monitoring physical and electromagnetic emanations from the information system and surrounding areas. |
| Supplemental Guidance | Information leakage may occur through electromagnetic emanations, acoustic emissions, vibrations, or other side channels. The organization uses technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information and the threat environment. |
