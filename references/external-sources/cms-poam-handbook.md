## POA&M Process Overview

Steps: Identify weaknesses → Develop a Corrective Action Plan (CAP) → Determine resource/funding availability → Assign a completion date → Execute the CAP → Verify weakness completion → Accept risk when applicable.

## Weakness Severity Levels (OMB-defined)

| Severity Level | Description |
|---|---|
| Significant deficiency | Drastically restricts agency mission capability or compromises security/privacy; requires immediate/near-immediate corrective action and senior management notification. |
| Reportable Condition | Affects efficiency/effectiveness of operations; lower risk, may be scheduled over longer period. |
| Weakness | All other findings; must be mitigated in a timely, efficient manner as resources permit. |

## Status Transition — Draft to Ongoing (30-Day Rule)

> "Once the CAP is documented, the plan must be entered into CFACTS in the form of a series of milestone records. **The status of the POA&M will automatically be moved from 'draft' to 'ongoing' 30 days after the weakness creation date.** Once a milestone has been accepted/approved and closed, the record must be retained for one year."

## Verification Cadence — At Least Quarterly

> "CMS requires that all information in the POA&M be updated **at least quarterly**, ensuring accuracy for efficient tracking and reporting."

Also: "Systems must review and update POA&Ms **at least quarterly**" (for weaknesses discovered during government audits), with compensating controls documented until remediation/mitigation.

## Risk-Based Decision (RBD) Review Cadence

> "After approval, RBDs shall be reviewed **at least annually** to ensure the risk remains acceptable and updated as events occur and information changes."

## Reporting Cadence — Monthly to HHS

> "CMS reports its remediation efforts on a monthly basis... CMS must submit POA&M updates **at least once a month (by the 3rd business day of each month)** to HHS to demonstrate the status of POA&M mitigation or remediation activities."

Completed POA&Ms are retained in reporting "for up to one year from the date of completion."

## Weakness Remediation and Mitigation Timeline — the Key Cadence Table

> "After positive identification of scan findings or approval of security assessment and/or audit report, all findings/weaknesses shall be documented in a POA&M, reported to HHS, and remediated/mitigated within the following remediation timelines."

| Severity | Remediation Timeline |
|---|---|
| Critical | Within **15 days** |
| High | Within **30 days** |
| Moderate | Within **90 days** |
| Low | Within **365 days** |

These timelines are based on the date the weakness is identified, **not** the date the POA&M is created. If additional time is needed, a justification with a modified estimated completion date must be documented; if not remediated by the scheduled date, status changes to "Delayed."

## Closing a POA&M

- Low/Moderate POA&Ms: closed by the ISSO, spot-audited by a Cyber Risk Advisor (CRA).
- Critical/High POA&Ms: closed by the CRA.
- Audit-generated POA&Ms: reviewed by the auditor prior to closure.
- Penetration-test POA&Ms: closed by the PenTest team after re-test.

## Annual Assessment (Glossary Definition)

> "Annual Assessment: The process of validating the effective implementation of security and privacy controls in the information system and its environment of operation **within every three hundred sixty-five (365) days**... to determine the extent to which the controls are implemented correctly, operating as intended, and producing the desired outcome."
