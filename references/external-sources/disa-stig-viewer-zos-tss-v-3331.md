## Finding V-3331 — z/OS TSS STIG (2017-03-22)

**Requirement:** Reviews and analysis of information system audit records are performed **every seven days or more frequently if required by the site Security Log Management policy**.

This finding maps to the broader NIST 800-53 AU-6 (Audit Review, Analysis, and Reporting) control family and demonstrates that, in practice, DISA STIG implementation guidance for z/OS systems using CA Top Secret Security (TSS) sets a concrete numeric floor — **7 days** — for how often audit records must be reviewed, consistent with the "at least weekly" cadence found in the FedRAMP and IRS Publication 1075 control baselines.
