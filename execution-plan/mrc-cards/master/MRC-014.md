## 5. Procedure

*Authored procedure (from `runbooks/ISSO.md`, not a generic pattern):*

1. In Splunk, monitor the security-incident correlation dashboard/SIEM alert queue (a scheduled saved search/alert per the [Splunk Enterprise Alerting Manual](https://help.splunk.com/en/splunk-enterprise/alert-and-respond/alerting-manual/9.1/create-alerts/create-scheduled-alerts)) continuously for indicators requiring incident handling under IR-4. — Expected result: monitoring confirmed active at all times, with no lapse in alert-queue coverage. — Capture: coverage/shift log and alert-queue screenshot. — Stop/continue: if a coverage gap occurs, stop and escalate to the ISSM immediately.
2. For each triggered alert, verify the CND Team logged a ticket in the incident-tracking system with classification, containment, eradication, and recovery actions per the Incident Response Plan and the incident-lifecycle stages described in [NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 2](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/SpecialPublications/NIST.SP.800-61r2.pdf). — Expected result: an incident record is opened with detection timestamp for every qualifying alert. — Capture: incident record ID. — Stop/continue: continue to [MRC-017](MRC-017.md) for status-update handling once an incident record is open.
3. Run the Splunk incident-monitoring saved search to confirm all open incidents are being tracked to closure and no alert aged past the SLA without action, per IR-5. — Expected result: no open incident/alert exceeds its SLA age without a logged action. — Capture: saved-search output listing open incidents and ages. — Stop/continue: if any incident is past SLA with no action, stop and escalate to the ISSM immediately.
4. Cross-check the incident ticket count and status against the Splunk alert count for the same period to confirm no alerts were dropped or missed. — Expected result: ticket count reconciles with alert count (or every discrepancy is explained). — Capture: reconciliation note. — Stop/continue: continue.
5. Confirm the ISSM was notified of any incident meeting reportable-incident criteria, per [JSIG §1.5.15(f)](../../reference/JSIG-source/section-1.5-roles-and-responsibilities.md). — Expected result: ISSM notification confirmed and timestamped for every qualifying incident. — Capture: notification timestamp/record. — Stop/continue: continue.
6. Close out and log the incident record once resolved, retaining incident-handling documentation per the organization's records-retention schedule (NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 2 notes a 3-year retention period under General Records Schedule 24 as a comparator). — Expected result: closure recorded with final disposition. — Capture: closed record. — Stop/continue: continue.
7. Export the Splunk incident dashboard and ticketing system status report and file them in the repository location specified in the Task Index alongside the reviewer's name and date. — Expected result: cycle record filed. — Capture: the exported dashboard/report and log entry. — Stop/continue: cycle complete.
