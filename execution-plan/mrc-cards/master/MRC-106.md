## 5. Procedure

**Pattern G — Retention / Disposal** (assignment from `runbooks/ISSM.md`)

1. Pull the incident-response ticketing/case management system's (or Splunk ES incident review's) configured record retention and archival policy for closed incident cases. — Expected result: the configured retention setting is confirmed at or above the GRS 24 3-year-after-follow-up standard ([NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 2](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/SpecialPublications/NIST.SP.800-61r2.pdf)). — Capture: the retention/archival policy configuration export. — Stop/continue: if configured retention is shorter than the GRS 24 standard, stop and escalate — this is a retention-policy gap.
2. Confirm the retention clock for each closed incident is calculated from the date all follow-up/corrective actions were completed — not the incident close date — by spot-checking case metadata fields, per the GRS 24 "3 years after all necessary follow-up actions have been completed" standard ([NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 2](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/SpecialPublications/NIST.SP.800-61r2.pdf)). — Expected result: the sampled cases show the retention clock keyed to follow-up completion date, not close date. — Capture: the spot-check comparison. — Stop/continue: if the system computes retention from the wrong date field, stop and escalate as a systemic retention-configuration finding.
3. Query the incident record repository for any case approaching or past the 3-year post-follow-up retention threshold and verify it has not been purged prematurely. — Expected result: a complete list of cases at/near the threshold, all still present and retrievable. — Capture: the query results. — Stop/continue: if any case cannot be found that should still be within the retention window, stop and escalate immediately — potential premature record loss.
4. Cross-check the system's automated purge/archival job logs against the records-management policy to confirm no incident record was deleted before its retention threshold was reached. — Expected result: purge-job logs show zero out-of-policy deletions. — Capture: the purge-log review. — Stop/continue: continue.
5. Archive incident records that have reached the 3-year threshold to long-term storage per the organization's records-management policy, and confirm archived records remain retrievable. — Expected result: every eligible record is archived and a retrieval spot-check succeeds. — Capture: archive confirmation and retrieval spot-check result. — Stop/continue: continue.
6. Document the retention verification results (record counts, oldest retained case, any purge-log discrepancies), obtain ISSM sign-off, and file the report in the incident response records repository with the reviewer's name and date. — Expected result: complete, signed cycle package filed. — Capture: the filed report. — Stop/continue: cycle complete.
