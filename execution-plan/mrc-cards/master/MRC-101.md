## 5. Procedure

**Pattern D — Test / Exercise** (assignment from `runbooks/PSO.md`)

1. Physically inspect all fire detection and suppression equipment in the SCIF/facility (smoke/heat detectors, pull stations, fire extinguishers, sprinkler or clean-agent suppression systems), recording each unit's inspection tag date and last-service date. — Expected result: a complete inventory of fire protection equipment with current inspection/service status for every unit. — Capture: the inspection checklist with tag dates/photos. — Stop/continue: if any unit lacks a current inspection tag, flag it as a finding now and continue the walk-through.
2. Coordinate with the fire alarm monitoring vendor or building engineering to trigger a functional test of the detection and suppression system, confirming the alarm signal reaches the monitoring station. — Expected result: a documented successful (or failed) end-to-end signal test. — Capture: the vendor/engineering test confirmation or work order. — Stop/continue: if the signal test fails to reach the monitoring station, stop and escalate immediately — this is a life-safety and facility-monitoring gap, not a routine finding.
3. Inspect the space for water-damage risk factors (overhead plumbing, sprinkler heads positioned above sensitive equipment, roof leaks, raised-floor moisture), including any area used to store backup media or backup appliance hardware for your backup solution (NetBackup media servers/storage units or Veeam Backup & Replication repositories) — critical backup media should sit in a separate, fire-rated storage area rather than under overhead plumbing or sprinkler risk per contingency-planning guidance ([GSA Contingency Planning Guide](https://www.gsa.gov/system/files/Contingency-Planning-(CP)-%5BCIO-IT-Security-06-29-Rev-7%5D.pdf)) — and photograph any deficiencies found. — Expected result: a documented list of water-damage risk factors and photos of any deficiency. — Capture: the annotated photo/finding set. — Stop/continue: if an active leak or standing water is found near operational or backup equipment, stop and escalate immediately.
4. Verify extinguisher inspection tags and suppression-system service records are current against the facility's fire protection service contract schedule. — Expected result: every unit's tag/service date is within its contracted service interval. — Capture: the service-record comparison. — Stop/continue: continue.
5. Document any deficiencies (failed detector, expired extinguisher, active leak) as findings and open a corrective action ticket in the local GRC/ticketing system with a remediation deadline. — Expected result: every deficiency has a tracking ID and owner. — Capture: ticket ID(s). — Stop/continue: continue.
6. Complete the fire protection/water damage inspection checklist, obtain FSO sign-off, and file it in the facility security records repository with the inspector's name and date per the Task Index. — Expected result: complete, signed cycle package filed. — Capture: the filed checklist package. — Stop/continue: cycle complete.
