## 5. Procedure

*Authored procedure — the generic "Pattern C" refresh/patch boilerplate does not fit an event-driven personnel-transfer action; superseded below with a sourced, control-specific procedure:*

1. Upon notification of a personnel transfer, verify the Personnel Security Officer initiated the transfer reassessment checklist per PS-5. — Expected result: checklist opened with a timestamp at time of notification. — Capture: personnel/HR system checklist entry. — Stop/continue: continue.
2. Using `Get-ADUser` and `Get-ADGroupMember`, confirm the individual's Active Directory group memberships and access rights were reassessed against the gaining position's need-to-know and reassigned/removed as appropriate within 24 hours. — Expected result: AD group memberships reflect the gaining position's need-to-know within the 24-hour window. — Capture: AD change timestamp/output. — Stop/continue: if the window is at risk of being missed, stop and escalate to the PSO/ISSM immediately.
3. Confirm access to systems, shares, or SCI/SAP compartments tied to the prior position that are no longer required were revoked, and any new access was granted only after gaining-position validation. — Expected result: no prior-position access remains that is not justified by the gaining position. — Capture: access-revocation/grant record. — Stop/continue: if unauthorized prior-position access is found still active, stop and escalate per the Escalation block below.
4. Cross-check facility/badge-access permissions were updated to reflect the new duty location or area via the badge-access system. — Expected result: badge-access permissions match the new duty location/area. — Capture: badge-system update record. — Stop/continue: continue.
5. Verify the timestamp of the AD access change against the transfer effective date to confirm the 24-hour requirement was met; this window is consistent with the 24-hour account-change notification comparator cited in [IRS Publication 1075](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p1075.pdf) for similarly sensitive federal information environments. — Expected result: elapsed time confirmed within 24 hours, or a missed-SLA finding is opened. — Capture: elapsed-time record. — Stop/continue: if the SLA was missed, open a finding and escalate per the Escalation block below.
6. Export the completed transfer reassessment checklist and AD group-membership change log and file them in the repository location specified in the Task Index alongside the reviewer's name and date. — Expected result: cycle record filed. — Capture: the exported checklist and log. — Stop/continue: cycle complete.
