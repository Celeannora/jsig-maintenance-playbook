## 5. Procedure

**Pattern F — Training Delivery** (assignment from `runbooks/ISSM.md`)

Applies to tasks that deliver recurring training (e.g., annual security awareness refresher, role-based training, incident response training, contingency training). No reference doc in `reference/external-sources/` specifies an AT-3 cadence or product; the Annual frequency and generic LMS/training-tracking-system language below are retained from the `curated_actions` skeleton and the Master Calendar rather than a cited external source.

1. Identify all personnel in role-based positions requiring specialized training (e.g., system administrators, ISSOs, privileged users) by cross-referencing job role/duty assignments against the Active Directory privileged-group membership (`Get-ADGroupMember`). — Expected result: a complete list of personnel in scope for role-based training this cycle. — Capture: the privileged-group membership export and role cross-reference, with pull date. — Stop/continue: if the AD export is incomplete, stop and remediate the collection gap before scoping the training population from partial data.
2. Assign the current-version role-based training module(s) applicable to each identified role in the organization's LMS/training-tracking system. — Expected result: content confirmed current, module(s) assigned to every identified individual per role. — Capture: content version/date and assignment record. — Stop/continue: continue.
3. Track and follow up on module completion for each assigned individual throughout the designated training period, issuing reminders for overdue personnel. — Expected result: reminders issued to every overdue individual before the period closes. — Capture: the reminder log. — Stop/continue: continue.
4. Export the LMS completion roster showing role, module completed, completion date, and pass status for each assigned individual. — Expected result: completion roster with pass/incomplete status per person and role. — Capture: the completion roster export. — Stop/continue: continue.
5. Identify personnel who did not complete role-based training by the deadline and notify the ISSM/ISSO to consider restricting elevated access pending completion. — Expected result: every non-completion has a documented notification and target follow-up date. — Capture: the notification log. — Stop/continue: escalate per the Escalation block immediately for any non-completion involving an active privileged-access holder, rather than waiting for the routine follow-up cycle.
6. Reconcile the role-based completion roster against the privileged-account list to confirm every privileged-role holder has a current-cycle completion record. — Expected result: zero unreconciled privileged accounts, or a documented exception list. — Capture: the reconciliation record. — Stop/continue: continue.
7. File the exported role-based training completion roster in the repository location specified in the Task Index with the Training Manager's name and reporting date. — Expected result: cycle record filed. — Capture: the filed roster. — Stop/continue: cycle complete.
