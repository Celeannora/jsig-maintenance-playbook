## 5. Procedure

*Authored procedure — the generic "Pattern C" refresh/patch boilerplate does not fit an event-driven personnel-termination action; superseded below with a sourced, control-specific procedure:*

1. Upon notification of personnel termination, verify the Personnel Security Officer initiated the termination checklist in the personnel/HR system per PS-4. — Expected result: checklist opened with a timestamp at time of notification. — Capture: personnel/HR system checklist entry. — Stop/continue: continue.
2. Confirm the System Administrator revoked the individual's Active Directory account (`Get-ADUser -Filter`, `Disable-ADAccount`) and removed group memberships (`Get-ADGroupMember`) within 24 hours. — Expected result: account disabled and group memberships removed within the 24-hour window. — Capture: AD change timestamp/output. — Stop/continue: if the window is at risk of being missed, stop and escalate to the PSO/ISSM immediately.
3. Confirm badge/facility access credentials were deactivated by pulling the badge-access system's deactivation log for the individual. — Expected result: badge/facility access shows deactivated status within the window. — Capture: badge-system deactivation log entry. — Stop/continue: continue.
4. Verify collection of government property, security debrief, and return of any cryptographic/authentication tokens is documented on the termination checklist. — Expected result: every checklist item is completed and documented. — Capture: completed termination checklist. — Stop/continue: if an item cannot be completed (e.g., unreturned token), stop and escalate per the Escalation block below.
5. Cross-check the timestamp of account disablement against the termination effective date/time to confirm the 24-hour requirement was met; this window is consistent with the 24-hour account-change notification comparator cited in [IRS Publication 1075](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p1075.pdf) for similarly sensitive federal information environments. — Expected result: elapsed time confirmed within 24 hours, or a missed-SLA finding is opened. — Capture: elapsed-time record. — Stop/continue: if the SLA was missed, open a finding and escalate per the Escalation block below.
6. Export the completed termination checklist and AD account-disablement log and file them in the repository location specified in the Task Index alongside the reviewer's name and date. — Expected result: cycle record filed. — Capture: the exported checklist and log. — Stop/continue: cycle complete.
