## 5. Procedure

**Pattern B — Manual Review of a List or Log** (assignment from `runbooks/PSO.md`). PE-8 requires visitor access records to be reviewed **at least monthly** and retained **for a minimum of one year**, with anomalies reported to the designated personnel ([FedRAMP SSP Appendix A — Moderate Baseline, PE-8](https://www.fedramp.gov/resources/templates/SSP-Appendix-A-Moderate-FedRAMP-Security-Controls.docx)), consistent with this card's Monthly frequency.

1. Pull the visitor register (electronic PACS visitor log or paper visitor log, per site configuration) for the review month. — Expected result: a complete, unaltered visitor-register export/copy covering every day of the review month. — Capture: the export/copy itself, with pull timestamp. — Stop/continue: if the register is incomplete (a gap in coverage), stop and remediate the logging/collection gap before reviewing partial data.
2. Verify each entry contains visitor name, organization, clearance/access authorization verification, escort name, and entry/exit timestamps. — Expected result: every entry is complete against this field set. — Capture: the annotated register noting any incomplete entries. — Stop/continue: continue regardless of completeness findings.
3. Confirm visitor entries requiring a signed non-disclosure or courier authorization have the supporting form on file. — Expected result: every applicable entry has a matched, filed authorization form. — Capture: the cross-reference list of entries checked against forms on file. — Stop/continue: continue.
4. Check that visitor records are retained per the facility's records-retention schedule and have not been purged early — PE-8 requires visitor access records be retained for a minimum of one year ([FedRAMP SSP Appendix A — Moderate Baseline, PE-8.a](https://www.fedramp.gov/resources/templates/SSP-Appendix-A-Moderate-FedRAMP-Security-Controls.docx)). — Expected result: confirmation that records covering at least the required retention window are present and intact. — Capture: retention-verification note. — Stop/continue: if records have been purged early, stop and escalate the retention gap immediately.
5. Reconcile a sample of visitor entries against escort sign-in sheets to confirm continuous escort coverage — restricted-area visitor activity must be escorted and controlled in all circumstances ([FedRAMP SSP Appendix A — Moderate Baseline, PE-3.d](https://www.fedramp.gov/resources/templates/SSP-Appendix-A-Moderate-FedRAMP-Security-Controls.docx)). — Expected result: every sampled entry shows continuous escort coverage for the full visit duration. — Capture: the sampled reconciliation worksheet. — Stop/continue: if any sampled entry shows an escort gap, stop and escalate immediately per the Escalation block.
6. Note any incomplete or missing entries and route to the Facility Security Officer for correction. — Expected result: every discrepancy has an assigned follow-up owner. — Capture: notification record/ticket. — Stop/continue: continue.
7. Export the visitor records review summary and file it in the repository location specified in the Task Index, alongside the reviewer's name and date. — Expected result: evidence package filed and retrievable for audit. — Capture: the filed export and its repository path. — Stop/continue: cycle complete.
