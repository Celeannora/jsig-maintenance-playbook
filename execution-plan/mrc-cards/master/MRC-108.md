## 5. Procedure

**Pattern G — Retention / Disposal** (assignment from `runbooks/Information-Owner-Steward.md`)

1. Pull the current data inventory/system of records listing all PII holdings and their assigned retention periods from the Data Owner's records schedule in the local GRC/document-repository system. — Expected result: a complete PII inventory with a retention period assigned to each holding. — Capture: the data inventory export. — Stop/continue: if any PII holding lacks an assigned retention period, stop and remediate the schedule gap before proceeding.
2. Cross-check the PII data sets against the organization's NARA-approved or agency-specific records disposition schedule to confirm correct retention periods are applied. — Expected result: every holding's assigned retention period matches its schedule entry. — Capture: the cross-check comparison. — Stop/continue: continue.
3. Query the storage location (file share, database, or records repository) for PII records that have reached or exceeded their scheduled retention period. — Expected result: a complete list of records eligible for disposal (may be empty). — Capture: the eligible-records query results. — Stop/continue: continue regardless of list size.
4. Execute or witness the disposal action (secure deletion, degauss, shred, or sanitization per NIST SP 800-88) for records past their retention period and capture a certificate of destruction or deletion log. — Expected result: disposal completed for every eligible record with documented method. — Capture: the certificate of destruction or deletion log. — Stop/continue: continue.
5. Confirm no PII record subject to a litigation hold or active investigation was included in the disposal action. — Expected result: zero held/investigated records included in the completed disposal batch. — Capture: the litigation-hold/investigation clearance check. — Stop/continue: if a held record was inadvertently included, stop and escalate immediately — potential spoliation issue requiring legal/ISSM involvement.
6. Document the retention/disposal actions taken, obtain Information Owner/Steward sign-off, and file the disposal log and certificate in the data governance records repository with the reviewer's name and date. — Expected result: complete, signed cycle package filed. — Capture: the filed disposal log/certificate package. — Stop/continue: cycle complete.
