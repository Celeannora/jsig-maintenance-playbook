## 5. Procedure

**Pattern F — Training Delivery** (assignment from `runbooks/ISSM.md`)

1. Pull the current contingency plan (CP) and the most recent backup/recovery test results from your backup solution's job console (NetBackup Activity Monitor/Jobs view or Veeam Backup & Replication job History — see [Veritas NetBackup Administrator's Guide](https://www.veritas.com/support/en_US/doc/150157642-165935937-0/v130722414-165935937) and [Veeam Backup & Replication User Guide](https://helpcenter.veeam.com/docs/vbr/userguide/session_results_web.html)) to identify any procedural changes since the last training cycle. — Expected result: current CP and recent job/session evidence in hand, with any procedural deltas noted. — Capture: CP version/date and the pulled job/session summary. — Stop/continue: continue.
2. Review contingency training content against the **two-year training-content refresh cycle** and the requirement to obtain evidence of contingency training at least annually ([GSA Contingency Planning (CP) Guide, "Contingency Training Refresh Cycle"](https://www.gsa.gov/system/files/Contingency-Planning-(CP)-%5BCIO-IT-Security-06-29-Rev-7%5D.pdf)) and the **at-least-annual** ISCP training cadence ([NIST SP 800-34 Rev. 1 §3.5.2](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/34/r1/final)); update content if it has not been refreshed within the two-year window or if procedures changed in step 1. — Expected result: content confirmed within its refresh window and reflecting current recovery procedures, or updated. — Capture: content version/date and refresh-cycle determination. — Stop/continue: continue.
3. Cross-reference the LMS enrollment roster against personnel assigned contingency roles (system administrators, backup operators, alternate-site staff) to identify who is due for the annual refresher. — Expected result: complete required-attendee roster. — Capture: roster export, with pull date. — Stop/continue: continue.
4. Deliver the contingency training, including a walkthrough of current backup/restore procedures and any updated recovery-time/recovery-point objectives. — Expected result: session(s) delivered to the identified population. — Capture: delivery date(s) and method. — Stop/continue: continue.
5. Incorporate at least one hands-on or simulated recovery exercise so trainees demonstrate familiarity with the actual recovery tooling — e.g., a walkthrough of the NetBackup Recovery page/Recovery wizard test-restore procedure, or a Veeam SureBackup verification job against an isolated virtual lab (per the two source guides cited above), whichever backup solution is deployed. — Expected result: trainees observe or perform a sample restore/verification workflow. — Capture: exercise notes and which tool/workflow was demonstrated. — Stop/continue: continue.
6. Export the LMS completion roster showing course name, completion date, and pass/fail status for each required attendee. — Expected result: complete completion roster. — Capture: the exported roster. — Stop/continue: continue.
7. File the completion roster and training materials in the training records repository with the Training Manager's name and sign-off date. — Expected result: cycle record filed. — Capture: the filed roster and materials. — Stop/continue: cycle complete.
