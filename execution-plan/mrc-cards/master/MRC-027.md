## 5. Procedure

**Pattern A — Automated Monitoring / Scan Confirmation** (assignment from `runbooks/ISSO.md`)

1. Launch the scheduled Nessus credentialed scan job (or confirm the automated scheduled job executed) against the full authorized IP/asset range for the month, per the credentialed-scan workflow in the [Tenable Nessus User Guide](https://docs.tenable.com/nessus/10_12/Content/PDF/Nessus_10_12.pdf), consistent with the monthly OS/web-application/database scan cadence in the [FedRAMP Continuous Monitoring Playbook](https://www.fedramp.gov/resources/documents/Continuous_Monitoring_Playbook.pdf). — Expected result: scan launched or confirmed running against the complete authorized scope. — Capture: scan-launch confirmation/screenshot with scope and timestamp. — Stop/continue: if the scheduled job did not run, stop and open a missed-cycle ticket in the local ticketing/GRC system before proceeding.
2. Confirm the scan used current plugin/signature definitions and completed without credential-failure or host-unreachable errors in the Nessus scan status log. — Expected result: scan status shows completed with no unexplained credential or connectivity errors, and the plugin feed date is current. — Capture: scan status/summary export. — Stop/continue: if a significant portion of the scope failed due to credential/connectivity errors, stop and remediate before treating the scan as representative.
3. Review the Nessus scan report for new critical/high findings and cross-check against WSUS patch deployment status for affected hosts. — Expected result: every critical/high finding is matched to a WSUS patch-approval/deployment status. — Capture: annotated findings-to-WSUS-status cross-reference. — Stop/continue: continue to step 4 regardless of findings; findings are documented, not gated, at this step.
4. Validate a sample of flagged vulnerabilities against actual host configuration to rule out false positives, noting any exceptions. — Expected result: sampled findings are confirmed true positive or documented as false positive with rationale. — Capture: validation notes for the sampled findings. — Stop/continue: if any true positive is CAT I (per STIG CAT rating, CVSS-derived CAT, or organizational policy), stop and escalate immediately per the Escalation block below before continuing routine triage.
5. Feed newly identified unresolved findings into the POA&M tracker per the CA-5 process (see [MRC-025](MRC-025.md)). — Expected result: every unresolved finding has an open POA&M tracking ID. — Capture: POA&M ID(s) for new entries. — Stop/continue: continue.
6. Export the Nessus scan report and file it in the repository location specified in the Task Index alongside the reviewer's name and date. — Expected result: cycle record filed. — Capture: the exported scan report. — Stop/continue: cycle complete.
