## 5. Procedure

**Pattern A — Automated Monitoring / Scan Confirmation** (assignment from `runbooks/ISSM.md`)

1. Coordinate with the certified TSCM team to schedule a physical and electronic Technical Surveillance Countermeasures Survey of the SCIF/sensitive space per the facility's TSCM policy interval. — Expected result: a confirmed survey date with a certified TSCM team on the calendar. — Capture: the scheduling confirmation/work order. — Stop/continue: if no certified TSCM team is available within the required interval, stop and escalate the scheduling gap to the PSO before the interval lapses.
2. Conduct the physical inspection of the space (walls, ceiling, furniture, fixtures) for unauthorized devices, wiring anomalies, or tampering evidence. — Expected result: a complete physical inspection record covering every area of the space. — Capture: the inspection checklist/notes. — Stop/continue: if a suspected device or tampering evidence is found, stop the routine survey and escalate immediately per Step 5.
3. Perform RF spectrum analysis and telephone/data line sweeps using TSCM-certified equipment to detect unauthorized transmitters or eavesdropping devices. — Expected result: a complete RF/line-sweep record with no unexplained signals, or a documented anomaly. — Capture: the RF spectrum/sweep data log. — Stop/continue: if an unexplained transmission or anomaly is detected, stop and escalate immediately per Step 5.
4. Cross-reference any scheduled Nessus scan results ([Tenable Nessus User Guide](https://docs.tenable.com/nessus/10_12/Content/PDF/Nessus_10_12.pdf)) for the facility's network segment covering the same survey window, to the extent the organization's Nessus deployment includes wireless/rogue-device discovery capability, for corroborating evidence of unauthorized wireless access points or rogue devices. — Expected result: Nessus results for the survey window are reviewed and any network-visible anomalies are cross-checked against the physical/RF findings. — Capture: the Nessus scan export reviewed. — Stop/continue: continue.
5. Document all findings, including any detected anomalies, in the TSCM survey report and immediately report confirmed technical penetrations to the ISSM/counterintelligence per incident procedures. — Expected result: every finding is documented, and any confirmed penetration is reported to the ISSM/counterintelligence without delay. — Capture: the TSCM survey report. — Stop/continue: a confirmed technical penetration is a CAT I event — escalate immediately per the Escalation block below.
6. Route the completed TSCM survey report through the PSO for review and retain it as a classified record, filing it in the facility security records repository with the surveyor's name and date. — Expected result: complete, reviewed, and properly classified/filed survey report. — Capture: the filed classified report. — Stop/continue: cycle complete.
