## 5. Procedure

**Pattern B — Manual Review of a List or Log** (assignment from `runbooks/ISSM.md`). The organization-level POA&M rollup should be updated **at least monthly**, matching FedRAMP's CA-5 "update the POA&M at least monthly" requirement and the [FedRAMP Continuous Monitoring Deliverables Template](https://www.fedramp.gov/resources/templates/FedRAMP-Continuous-Monitoring-Deliverables-Template.xlsx)'s Monthly POA&M/executive-summary deliverable cadence; the underlying subordinate-system POA&Ms feeding this rollup are themselves reviewed/updated at least quarterly per the [CMS POA&M Handbook](https://security.cms.gov/learn/cms-plan-action-and-milestones-poam-handbook).

1. Pull the current POA&M items from each subordinate system's individual POA&M tracker in the GRC/ticketing repository. — Expected result: a complete, current export of every subordinate system's open, overdue, and newly closed POA&M items. — Capture: the export file(s), with pull timestamp. — Stop/continue: if any subordinate system's tracker is unreachable or its export is incomplete, stop and remediate the collection gap before rolling up partial data.
2. Consolidate open, overdue, and newly closed POA&M items into the organization-level POA&M rollup register. — Expected result: a single, consolidated rollup register current as of this cycle. — Capture: the updated rollup register. — Stop/continue: continue.
3. Verify each open item still has an assigned resource, milestone dates, and interim mitigation status; flag items past their milestone date as overdue — remediation timelines are severity-based (Critical 15 days / High 30 days / Moderate 90 days / Low 365 days, measured from the date the weakness was identified, not the POA&M creation date) per the [CMS POA&M Handbook](https://security.cms.gov/learn/cms-plan-action-and-milestones-poam-handbook). — Expected result: every open item is dispositioned as on-track or overdue against its severity-based timeline. — Capture: the annotated rollup with overdue flags. — Stop/continue: continue regardless of overdue count, but proceed to step 5 for any overdue high-severity item.
4. Recalculate aggregate risk exposure/metrics (e.g., count of overdue items by severity) for the organization-level risk view. — Expected result: updated aggregate metrics reflecting this cycle's data. — Capture: the metrics summary. — Stop/continue: continue.
5. Brief the ISSM and AO/DAO on overdue or high-risk POA&M items requiring escalation or resource reallocation. — Expected result: overdue/high-risk items have been briefed and any needed resource decisions requested. — Capture: briefing record/notes. — Stop/continue: if a Critical or High-severity item is past its remediation timeline, escalate immediately per the Escalation block rather than waiting for the next cycle.
6. Update the Risk Executive Function notification record for items affecting organizational risk posture. — Expected result: Risk Executive Function notified for every applicable item. — Capture: notification record. — Stop/continue: continue.
7. Export the updated organization-level POA&M rollup and file it in the repository location specified in the Task Index, alongside the reviewer's name and date. — Expected result: evidence package filed and retrievable for audit. — Capture: the filed export and its repository path. — Stop/continue: cycle complete.
