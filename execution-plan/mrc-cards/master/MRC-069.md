## 5. Procedure

**Pattern B — Manual Review of a List or Log** (assignment from `runbooks/Privileged-Users.md`)

1. Run an ePO Asset/Software Inventory report (Menu > Reporting > Queries & Reports, Managed Systems result type) ([Trellix ePolicy Orchestrator Product Guide](http://b2b-download.trellix.com/PRODUCTS/naibeta-download/epo_460/previous%20postings/epo_460_beta2_productguide.pdf)), or `Get-ADComputer -Filter * -Properties *` for domain-joined systems, to export the current list of all computers and endpoints on the SAP network. — Expected result: a complete, unaltered export covering every managed/domain-joined system. — Capture: the export file, with pull timestamp. — Stop/continue: if the export is incomplete (gap in coverage), stop and remediate the collection gap before reviewing partial data.
2. Pull the physical removable-media/hardware accountability log for all classified removable media and portable devices issued to the program, including any media logged as backup tapes/backup-media sets. — Expected result: a current, complete accountability log for the review period. — Capture: the log export. — Stop/continue: continue.
3. Reconcile the ePO/AD system inventory and the removable-media log against the CCB-approved hardware baseline list to identify unauthorized, missing, or unaccounted-for items. — Expected result: every inventoried item and logged media entry reconciles to the CCB baseline. — Capture: the reconciliation worksheet. — Stop/continue: continue regardless of discrepancy count; log every mismatch for step 5.
4. Physically spot-check a sample of listed devices/media against their logged location to confirm accountability records are accurate; for any BitLocker-protected device in the sample, also confirm reported protection status via `manage-bde -status`/`Get-BitLockerVolume` ([Microsoft Learn BitLocker Operations Guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/security/operating-system-security/data-protection/bitlocker/operations-guide)). — Expected result: every sampled item is physically located where logged, with correct protection status. — Capture: the spot-check worksheet, including command output for BitLocker-protected samples. — Stop/continue: if a sampled item cannot be located or its protection status contradicts the log, stop and escalate immediately per the Escalation block — this is a potential lost/compromised classified-media finding, not a routine discrepancy.
5. Document discrepancies (unauthorized additions, missing items, custody gaps) and route them to the ISSM and CCB for investigation and correction. — Expected result: every discrepancy has a documented investigation owner and corrective action. — Capture: the discrepancy log with routing/ticket ID(s). — Stop/continue: continue.
6. Export the reconciled inventory report and file it in the repository location specified in the Task Index, alongside the reviewer's name and date. — Expected result: complete evidence package filed for the cycle. — Capture: the filed report. — Stop/continue: cycle complete.
