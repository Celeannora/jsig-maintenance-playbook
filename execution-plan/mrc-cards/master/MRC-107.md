## 5. Procedure

**Pattern G — Retention / Disposal** (assignment from `runbooks/PSO.md`)

1. Pull the visitor access control log (physical sign-in register or electronic badge-access system export) for the facility covering the applicable retention window. — Expected result: a complete export covering the full window under review. — Capture: the log export with pull timestamp. — Stop/continue: if the export shows a coverage gap, stop and remediate the logging/collection gap before reviewing partial data.
2. Verify the visitor log capture fields (name, organization, sponsor, date/time in-out, escort) are complete for the sampled period. — Expected result: every sampled entry has all required fields populated. — Capture: the field-completeness check results. — Stop/continue: continue.
3. Confirm the retention period configured in the badge-access system or physical log storage meets or exceeds the applicable source requirement — 5 years per [IRS Publication 1075](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p1075.pdf)'s PE-8 provision, used here as the top of this card's cited 1–5-year range. — Expected result: configured retention is confirmed at or above the applicable minimum. — Capture: the retention-configuration screenshot/export. — Stop/continue: if configured retention is shorter than required, stop and escalate — this is a retention-policy gap.
4. Spot-check that visitor records from the earliest required retention year are still retrievable and have not been destroyed prematurely, consistent with the at-least-monthly visitor-log review practice in [IRS Publication 1075](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p1075.pdf). — Expected result: the oldest in-scope records are retrievable and intact. — Capture: the spot-check retrieval result. — Stop/continue: if records from the required window cannot be retrieved, stop and escalate immediately — potential premature record loss.
5. Identify any visitor records that have exceeded the required retention period and route them for authorized destruction per records-management policy. — Expected result: a disposition candidate list (may be empty) routed for authorization. — Capture: the candidate list. — Stop/continue: if any candidate is under legal hold or active investigation, remove it from the disposition list and re-check at the next cycle.
6. Document the visitor access records retention check, obtain FSO sign-off, and file it in the physical security records repository with the reviewer's name and date. — Expected result: complete, signed cycle package filed. — Capture: the filed report. — Stop/continue: cycle complete.
