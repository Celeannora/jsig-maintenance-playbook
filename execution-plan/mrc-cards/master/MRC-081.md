## 5. Procedure

**Pattern F — Training Delivery** (assignment from `runbooks/ISSM.md`)

1. Pull the current-year incident response training curriculum from the organization's LMS/training-tracking system and confirm it reflects the latest approved IR plan version and lessons-learned items from the past cycle's incidents, per the **at-least-annual** plan/training review cadence in [NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 2 §2.3.2](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/61/r2/final). — Expected result: curriculum confirmed current, or updated to reflect plan/lessons-learned changes. — Capture: curriculum version/date and a note of any content changes made. — Stop/continue: continue.
2. Cross-reference the LMS enrollment roster against the list of personnel holding incident-handling roles (IR team, help desk, ISSO staff) to identify who is due for the annual refresher. — Expected result: a complete required-attendee roster for this cycle. — Capture: the roster export, with pull date. — Stop/continue: continue.
3. Schedule and deliver the refresher session (live or LMS-hosted module), including tabletop-style incident scenarios relevant to the system. — Expected result: session(s) delivered to the identified population. — Capture: delivery date(s) and method (live session vs. LMS module). — Stop/continue: continue.
4. Export the LMS completion roster showing course name, completion date, and pass/fail status for each required attendee. — Expected result: a complete completion roster covering every required attendee. — Capture: the exported completion roster. — Stop/continue: continue.
5. Follow up individually with any personnel showing incomplete or overdue status and re-assign the module with a new due date; document each follow-up. — Expected result: every incomplete/overdue attendee has a documented follow-up action and new target date. — Capture: the follow-up log. — Stop/continue: if a non-completion involves personnel with active incident-handling/privileged-access duties, escalate per the Escalation block rather than waiting for the routine follow-up cycle.
6. File the completion roster and session materials in the training records repository with the Training Manager's name and sign-off date. — Expected result: cycle record filed and retrievable for the next audit/assessment cycle. — Capture: the filed roster and materials. — Stop/continue: cycle complete.
