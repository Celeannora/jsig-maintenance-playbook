### Operational runbook / SOP structure and cadence

| Section / Step | Explicit content |
|---|---|
| Chapter Three process | prepare for security and privacy control assessments; develop security and privacy assessment plans; conduct security and privacy control assessments; analyze assessment report results; assess security and privacy capabilities |
| Table 1: Prepare step purpose | address a range of issues pertaining to the cost, schedule, scope, and performance of the control assessment |
| Table 1: Primary roles | Authorizing Official; Authorizing Official Designated Representative; system owner; common control provider; control assessors |
| Table 1: Supporting roles | System security officer; system privacy officer |
| Table 1: Outcomes | The objective, scope, and timeframe of control assessment determined; Key organizational stakeholders notified and necessary resources allocated; Assessors/assessment teams identified; Artifacts collected and provided to assessors/assessment teams; Mechanism to minimize ambiguities and misunderstandings about control implementation and identified control deficiencies or weaknesses established; Assessors/assessment teams understand the organization’s operations, structure, objective, scope, and timeframe of control assessment |
| Preparation activities by organization | ensuring appropriate policies covering security and privacy control assessments are in place and understood by all affected organizational elements; ensuring all steps in the RMF prior to the security or privacy control assessment step have been successfully completed and received appropriate management oversight; establishing objective and scope of assessments; ensuring common controls and common portion of hybrid controls assigned to appropriate organizational entities (common control providers) for development and implementation; notifying key organizational officials of impending assessments and allocating necessary resources; establishing appropriate communication channels among organizational officials with an interest in the assessments; establishing time frames for completing the assessments and key milestone decision points required by the organization; identifying and selecting appropriate assessors/assessment teams and considering issues of assessor independence; providing artifacts to assessors/assessment teams; establishing a mechanism between the organization and the assessors and/or assessment teams to minimize ambiguities or misunderstandings about implementation and related weaknesses or deficiencies |
| Preparation activities by assessors/assessment teams | obtaining a general understanding of the organization’s operations, including mission, functions, and business processes; obtaining an understanding of the structure of the system and the security and privacy controls being assessed; identifying the organizational entities responsible for development and implementation of the common controls or common portion of hybrid controls; meeting with appropriate organizational officials to ensure common understanding for assessment objectives and proposed rigor and scope; obtaining artifacts needed for the assessment; establishing appropriate organizational points of contact; obtaining previous assessment results that may be reused |
| Reuse / prior evidence | organizations are encouraged to take advantage of control assessment results and associated assessment documentation, artifacts, and evidence available for system components from previous assessments, including independent third-party testing, evaluation, and validation; previous assessment results may be appropriately reused for the current assessment; examples include Inspector General reports, audits, vulnerability scans, physical security inspections, developmental testing and evaluation, vendor flaw remediation activities, [ISO 15408] evaluations |
| Assessment plan inputs | information about controls to be assessed, including results from assessments of risk; platform-specific dependencies in hardware, software, or firmware; assessment procedures needed as a result of organization-specific controls not included in [SP 800-53] |
| Cadence / frequency | the frequency of assessments and monitoring is determined by the organization, system owner, and/or common control provider and is approved by the authorizing official; security and privacy controls are assessed on an ongoing basis in accordance with ISCM strategy and privacy continuous monitoring strategy |
| End-of-life / disposal | security and privacy assessments are conducted to ensure important organizational information, including personally identifiable information, are purged from the system prior to disposal and organizational retention schedules are adhered to |

### Recurring assessment methods, object types, and template headings

| Heading / template field | Explicit content |
|---|---|
| Assessment procedure structure | assessment objective; potential assessment methods and objects; determine if statements; Examine / Interview / Test methods |
| Assessment objects | specifications; mechanisms; activities; individuals |
| Assessment methods | examine; interview; test |
| Examine method | reviewing, inspecting, observing, studying, or analyzing one or more assessment objects to facilitate assessor understanding, achieve clarification, or obtain evidence |
| Interview method | holding discussions with individuals or groups of individuals within an organization to facilitate assessor understanding, achieve clarification, or obtain evidence |
| Test method | exercising one or more assessment objects under specified conditions to compare the actual state of the object to the desired state or expected behavior of the object |
| Depth attribute values | basic; focused; comprehensive |
| Coverage attribute values | basic; focused; comprehensive |
| ODP numbering convention | “XX-nn_ODP”; if more than one ODP for the assessment procedure, “_ODP” is followed by square-bracketed sequential numbers starting from “01” |
| ODP reference style | unique identifier and phrase “<... SELECTED PARAMETER VALUE(S)>” surrounded by < > |
| Assignment operations | organization defines a value such as frequency, circumstances, personnel, or roles |
| Selection operations | organization selects one or more options provided in the ODP |
| Enhancement numbering | sequential parenthetical numbers such as AC-17(01); assessment objectives numbered sequentially with bracketed numbers for further granularity |
