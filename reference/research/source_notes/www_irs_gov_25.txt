- **Purpose / overall objective**
  - “To measure the level of compliance with Federal disclosure regulations as defined in Pub 1075 and as documented in agency policies and procedures.”

- **Instructions / recurring compliance task execution**
  - Agencies are required to conduct internal inspections as part of their compliance with Publication 1075.
  - The questions serve as an agency internal inspection checklist to identify security procedures and federal security implementation for protecting Federal Tax Information.
  - Document provides:
    - High level questions related directly to Pub 1075 requirements
    - Pub 1075 references
    - Pass/Fail/NA: To notate agency compliance
    - Notes: Location for additional comments specific to the question

- **RACI / role responsibilities**
  - Official Conducting Internal Inspection
    - submits Internal Inspection Report to the headquarters function of this agency as part of the IRS Safeguards Internal Inspections requirement
  - Head of Location Being Reviewed
    - acknowledges review of the Internal Inspection Report
    - initiates appropriate corrective actions for any deficiencies identified
  - Agency Reviewer
    - acknowledges review of the Internal Inspection Report

- **Phases / sections / objectives**
  - I. Preliminary
    - Objective: To obtain a general understanding of safeguarding of Federal Taxpayer Information (FTI) in areas being reviewed.
  - II. Record Keeping Requirements (Publication 1075 section 2.A) IRC Section 6103 p 4 A
    - Objective: To ascertain that adequate policies, procedures, and systems are in place to identify, store, protect, and track FTI from receipt to destruction.
  - III. Secure Storage (Publication 1075 section 2.B) IRC Section 6103 p 4 B
    - Objective: To ascertain adequate security of the building or section of building where the FTI is located.
  - IV. Restricting Access (Publication 1075 section 2.C) IRC Section 6103(p)(4)(C)
    - Objective: To determine whether access to FTI is adequately controlled and restricted.
  - V. Disposing Federal Taxpayer Information Publication 1075 section 2.F IRC Section 6103 p 4 F
    - Objective: To determine if FTI is disposed of properly.
  - VI. Cybersecurity Requirements
    - Objective: To determine if information technology security and privacy requirements are met to adequately protect FTI.
  - VI. Conclusion
    - A. The agency must implement a process for ensuring that a POA&M is developed and monitored in accordance with Pub 1075.
    - B. Complete Internal Inspection Report
    - C. Retain Internal Inspection Report for 5 years
