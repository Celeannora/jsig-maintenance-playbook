- **Scope / use statements:**
  - This checklist does not replace the AO/DAO-approved SSP or SCTM for a particular system.
  - Evidence of system specific continuous monitoring results for a system can be provided to satisfy these self-assessment requirements if the scope of continuous monitoring assessments includes the items on this checklist.
  - This checklist serves as an aid for the inspection and assessment of information systems, networks, and components under the purview of the Department of Defense (DoD) Special Access Program Central Office (SAPCO) and DoD Service/Agency SAPCOs.
  - Based on the criteria specified within the JSIG, this checklist may be used in conjunction with the SAP Security Compliance Checklist in preparation for formal inspections and staff assistance visits.

- **Instructions / exception handling fields:**
  - Check Yes or No based on self-assessment of the organization and/or system(s) implementation.
  - Enter "S" for SCTM into the Yes column if deviations from the JSIG requirements exist that are approved in the SCTM.
  - Enter "P" for POA&M in the No column if deviations are documented in a POA&M that has been accepted by the AO.
  - For Non-Tailorable controls if "No" then indicate in the remarks if the Senior AO for the system has endorsed a waiver.
