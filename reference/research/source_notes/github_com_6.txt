- **Operational runbook / SOP structure**
  - This site documents parts of the PagerDuty Incident Response process.
  - It is a cut-down version of internal documentation.
  - Used at PagerDuty for any major incidents.
  - Used to prepare new employees for on-call responsibilities.
  - Provides information on:
    - preparing for an incident
    - what to do during an incident
    - what to do after an incident
  - Described as a collection of pages detailing how to efficiently deal with any major incidents that might arise.
  - Includes information on how to go on-call effectively.
  - Includes lessons learned the hard way.
  - Includes training material for getting you up to speed quickly.
  - Intended for on-call practitioners and those involved in an operational incident response process, or those wishing to enact a formal incident response process.
  - Covered topics explicitly listed:
    - preparing to go on-call
    - definitions of severities
    - incident call etiquette
    - how to run a postmortem
    - postmortem template
    - security incident response process
