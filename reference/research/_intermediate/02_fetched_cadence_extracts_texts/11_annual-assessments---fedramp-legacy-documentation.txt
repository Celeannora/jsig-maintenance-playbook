## Additional explicit timelines / references

- **“within the same annual period”**  
  - Source section: **Define the Assessment Scope**  
  - Context: “CSP-selected controls required to address system changes that have been implemented and/or changed by a CSP since their last assessment (**this excludes those controls or portions of controls previously assessed under a significant change within the same annual period**)”  
  - Timeline: **within the same annual period**  
  - Likely family/control ID: **CA / CM**

- **“since the last assessment”**  
  - Source section: **Define the Assessment Scope**  
  - Context: “Validation of POA&Ms closed since the last assessment”  
  - Timeline: **since the last assessment**  
  - Likely family/control ID: **CA / CM**

- **“at least once in a three year period”**  
  - Source section: **Define the Assessment Scope**  
  - Context: controls not assessed within that period must be included to meet periodicity requirements  
  - Timeline: **three-year period**  
  - Likely family/control ID: **CA / CM / AU / SI / IA / AC**

- **“during the monthly collaborative ConMon meeting”**  
  - Source section: **Brief Agency Customers on Assessment Plan**  
  - Timeline: **monthly**  
  - Likely family/control ID: **CA / CM**

## Explicit absence of specific cadence

- **Develop the Assessment Schedule**: contains “timeframes and resources” but **no specific frequency** is stated.
- **Periodic Updates**: explicitly says FedRAMP “periodically” publishes updates, but **no specific cadence** (daily/weekly/monthly/quarterly/annually/etc.) is stated.
- **Conduct the Security Assessment and Develop SAR**: mentions building in time for draft SAR, remediation, remediation testing, and final SAR development, but **no specific cadence** is stated.
- **Complete POA&M**: requires preparation/submission and documentation of residual risks, but **no specific cadence** is stated.
