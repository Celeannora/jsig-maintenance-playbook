### Document scope / absence of NIST SP 800-53 Rev 4 / RMF / JSIG style maintenance schedules
- **No explicit NIST SP 800-53 Rev 4 / RMF / JSIG maintenance cadence schedules are documented in this page.**
- The source is **NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 2 (Computer Security Incident Handling Guide)**, and it provides **incident-response operational guidance**, not control-implementation frequencies for **MA, CM, AU, SI, CA, CP, IR, PE, AC, IA** controls.
- Where timing appears, it is generally **incident-response process timing** or **organization-defined / risk-based** language rather than fixed control-maintenance cadences.

## Additional explicit timing / retention details

- **Review incident response documentation and procedures at designated intervals**
  - Quote: “the incident response team should review all related documentation and procedures for handling incidents at designated intervals.”
  - **No specific interval is given.**

- **Log retention policy duration is organization-defined / data-retention dependent**
  - Quote: “The length of time to maintain log data is dependent on several factors, including the organization’s data retention policies and the volume of data.”
  - **No fixed cadence is given.**

- **Evidence retention is organization-defined**
  - Quote: “Organizations should establish policy for how long evidence from an incident should be retained.”
  - The page says most organizations retain evidence for **months or years**, and some cases **several years**.
  - **No exact universal retention period is mandated in this document.**

- **Lessons learned timing**
  - Quote: “The meeting should be held within several days of the end of the incident.”
  - Also: “Lessons learned meetings can also be held periodically for lesser incidents as time and resources permit.”
  - **No fixed periodic frequency is given for lesser incidents.**

- **Status updates**
  - Quote: policy should specify “what must be reported to whom and at what times (e.g., initial notification, regular status updates).”
  - **No fixed cadence is defined.**

- **Training frequency**
  - The page says the incident response plan should indicate “how often incident handlers should be trained,” but **does not specify a frequency**.

- **Periodic risk assessments**
  - Quote: “Periodic risk assessments of systems and applications should determine what risks are posed…”
  - **No exact period is given.**

- **Ongoing / continuous**
  - “Continuously monitored” host security.
  - “Continually monitoring for attacks is essential.”
  - **No exact interval is given.**

## Control-family mapping notes explicitly supported by the page
- **IR**: incident response policy, plan, procedures, coordination, notification, escalation, lessons learned, evidence handling, retention, reporting.
- **AU**: logging, auditing, log retention, log review, timestamps, evidence in logs.
- **SI**: monitoring, intrusion detection/prevention, malware detection, SIEM, alerting.
- **CM**: configuration/baseline-related language appears only indirectly via host hardening, configuration checklists, and router/firewall rulesets; **no explicit cadence** stated.
- **MA / CA / CP / PE / AC / IA**: **no explicit maintenance cadence/frequency/timeline requirements documented on this page** beyond general incident-response-adjacent process advice.
