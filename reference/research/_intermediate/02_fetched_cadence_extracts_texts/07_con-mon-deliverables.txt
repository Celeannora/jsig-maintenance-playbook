## Additional cadence/timeline details explicitly stated in the page

- **Monthly**
  - Continuous Monitoring Monthly Executive Summary
  - Vulnerability and Configuration Scanning
  - POA&M
  - Inventory

- **Annually**
  - Contingency Plan Test Plan/Test Results for Moderate and High
  - SSP and Supporting Documents Update
  - Static Code Analysis Methodology for Moderate and High
  - SAP
  - Penetration Testing (High and Moderate / Low and Li-SaaS)
  - Penetration Testing - Red Team Test Plan/Report for Moderate and High
  - Vulnerability and Configuration Scanning (3PAO/oversight version)
  - SAR

- **Every six (6) months**
  - Incident Response Test Plan/Test Results for High systems: “At least every six (6) months, including functional at least annually”

- **Every 3 years**
  - Contingency Plan Test Plan/Test Results for Low: “Classroom exercise/table top written test, at least every 3 years”

- **Within 10 business days**
  - Complete worksheet for CSPs pending authorization

- **Within 30 days prior to annual assessment activities**
  - SSP and Supporting Documents Update
  - Static Code Analysis Methodology / ConMon plan submission to 3PAO

- **Ad hoc**
  - Security Alerts, Advisories, and Directives

- **Risk-/org-defined / no fixed cadence stated**
  - Collaborative Continuous Monitoring: “CSP defined, in accordance with the FedRAMP Collaborative ConMon Quick Guide”
  - The instructions also say to coordinate with AO(s) on updates needed after initial submission and on initial submission schedule, but do not specify a fixed frequency beyond the annual update requirement for the worksheet.
