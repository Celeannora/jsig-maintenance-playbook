- **Daily / weekly / monthly / quarterly / annually / every X days / within X days / retention period specifics explicitly stated**
  - **Daily**
    - “daily or weekly” as examples of backup frequency in backup policy. **Likely control:** CP-9. Source: 3.4.2.
    - “System administrators responsible for maintaining daily information system operations.” **Not a cadence requirement; role description.** Source: 1.3.
  - **Weekly**
    - “daily or weekly” backup frequency examples. **Likely control:** CP-9. Source: 3.4.2.
    - Example report generation step: “Select Weekly.” **Test example, not operational cadence.** Source: Appendix A.1/A.2/A.3 Appendix E.
  - **Monthly**
    - No explicit monthly cadence stated.
  - **Quarterly**
    - No explicit quarterly cadence stated.
  - **Annually**
    - “reviewed annually and updated as necessary” in sample policy. **Likely control:** CP-1 / CP-2. Source: 3.1.
    - “tested annually” in sample policy. **Likely control:** CP-4. Source: 3.1.
    - “Training should be provided at least annually.” **Likely control:** CP-3. Source: 3.5.2.
    - “will be tested at least annually.” **Likely control:** CP-4. Source: A.1/A.2/A.3 Plan Approval.
    - “at the organization defined frequency (e.g., yearly)” for review/testing schedules. **Likely control:** CP-2 / CP-4. Sources: A.1 Appendix I; A.2 Appendix J; A.3 Appendix J.
  - **Every 30 days / 60 days / 90 days / 365 days**
    - No explicit 30/60/90/365-day cadence stated for maintenance, backup, testing, or review.
  - **Within X days / hours**
    - “within 12 hours” for COOP functions. **COOP-related, not SP 800-53 cadence.** Source: 2.2.2; 3.2.1; Appendix C.
    - “up to 30 days” for COOP functions. **COOP-related.** Source: 2.2.2; Appendix C.
    - “within 24 hours” for mobile sites delivery. **Operational logistics.** Source: 3.4.3.
    - “30 to 60 minutes” UPS backup power. **Operational duration.** Source: 5.1.3.
    - “several hours to several days” warm-site recovery; “several days to weeks” cold-site recovery. **Operational duration.** Source: 5.1.5.
    - “at the next scheduled backup period” for full system backup after recovery. **Likely control:** CP-9. Sources: A.1 §5.6; A.2 §5.7; A.3 §5.8.
  - **Retention period**
    - No explicit retention period stated for backups, logs, documentation, or records.

- **Control-frequency mapping where stated**
  - **CP-1**: formal contingency planning policy/procedures; review/update at organization-defined frequency. Source: Appendix E CP-1.
  - **CP-2**: contingency plan; review at organization-defined frequency; coordinate with related plans. Source: Appendix E CP-2.
  - **CP-3**: contingency training; refresher training at organization-defined frequency; at least annually in guide. Source: Appendix E CP-3; 3.5.2.
  - **CP-4**: contingency plan testing/exercises; frequency organization-defined; guide gives annual examples and TT&E schedules. Source: Appendix E CP-4; 3.5.4; Appendix A templates.
  - **CP-6**: alternate storage site; no cadence specified. Source: Appendix E CP-6.
  - **CP-7**: alternate processing site; no cadence specified, but site readiness/agreements and recovery time period apply. Source: Appendix E CP-7.
  - **CP-8**: telecommunications services; no cadence specified, but contracts/agreements and recovery time period apply. Source: Appendix E CP-8.
  - **CP-9**: information system backup; backups at organization-defined frequency consistent with RTO/RPO; tests of backups at organization-defined frequency. Source: Appendix E CP-9.
  - **CP-10**: recovery/reconstitution; no explicit cadence, but known secure state, failover, and restoration time periods. Source: Appendix E CP-10.

- **Explicit absence statements in the source**
  - “Contingency Plan Update (Withdrawn)” — CP-5 is withdrawn. Source: Table 2-1 / Table E-1 / Appendix E.
  - No specific frequency is given for:
    - contingency plan review/update beyond “organization-defined frequency,” “annually,” “periodically,” “regularly,” or “more often” for certain systems;
    - backups beyond “daily or weekly” examples and “organization-defined frequency consistent with recovery time and recovery point objectives”;
    - testing beyond “organization-defined frequency” and yearly examples;
    - retention periods for backups/logs/media.
