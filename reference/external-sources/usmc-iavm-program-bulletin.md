## Background

The Department of Defense (DoD) IAVM Program is designed to provide positive control of the vulnerability notification and corrective action process within DoD information systems. The Joint Task Force Global Network Operations (JTF-GNO) manages the execution of the DoD IAVM Program. The Marine Corps Network Operations and Security Center (MCNOSC) is the service component to JTF-GNO and the USMC reporting agent for the DoD IAVM Program.

## Monthly Vulnerability Scanning Requirement

**Command/Local Information Assurance Managers (IAMs):**
> "(5) CONDUCT MONTHLY VULNERABILITY SCANS IAW REF H. IMMEDIATELY UPON COMPLETION OF SCANS REMEDIATE ALL OPEN VULNERABILITIES AND CORRECT CONFIGURATION ISSUES."

**Systems Administrators/Green/Blue Team Members:**
> "(7) CONDUCT MONTHLY VULNERABILITY SCANS OF ALL COMMAND ASSETS PER REF H."

**NMCI Vendor (EDS):**
> "(5) CONDUCT MONTHLY VULNERABILITY SCANS IAW REF H. IMMEDIATELY UPON COMPLETION OF SCANS REMEDIATE ALL OPEN VULNERABILITIES AND CORRECT CONFIGURATION ISSUES."

## Quarterly Wireless Assessment Requirement

**Systems Administrators/Green/Blue Team Members:**
> "(3) CONDUCT QUARTERLY WAR-DRIVING WIRELESS DETECTION ASSESSMENTS AND REPORT ROGUE DEVICES TO MCNOSC VMT."

## POA&M Scan Freshness Requirement — 14-Day Maximum

> "(E) POA&M REQUEST CONTENT. POA&MS WILL ONLY BE ACCEPTED FOR REVIEW IF COMPLETED USING THE MOST CURRENT POA&M TEMPLATE... SPECIFIC ASSETS MUST BE IDENTIFIED IN THE POA&M REQUEST BY MACHINE NAME AND IP ADDRESS. A COMPLETE SCAN OF EACH ASSET MUST BE PROVIDED WITH THE POA&M. **THE SCAN MUST BE NO OLDER THAN 14 DAYS.**"

## IAVM Message Severity Tiers

1. **Information Assurance Vulnerability Alert (IAVA):** Generated when a new vulnerability poses an immediate and significant threat to DoD. JTF-GNO requires acknowledgement of receipt and compliance reporting. IAVAs do not expire until directed by JTF-GNO; compliance must be continuously maintained on all affected systems until they are removed from service or replaced/upgraded.
2. **Information Assurance Vulnerability Bulletin (IAVB):** Addresses new vulnerabilities that do not pose an immediate threat but carry potential for escalation. Compliance is mandatory; only acknowledgement of receipt is required.
3. **Technical Advisory (TA):** Addresses low-risk vulnerabilities, provided for informational purposes only; no acknowledgement required.

## Extension / Deadline Policy

> "(C) EXTENSION REQUESTS. EXTENSIONS ARE NOT AUTHORIZED IN THE DOD VULNERABILITY MANAGEMENT PROGRAM. It is recognized that operational requirements may occasionally prevent commands from achieving compliance within prescribed deadlines. The POA&M process... is meant to account for assets that cannot be patched on time."
