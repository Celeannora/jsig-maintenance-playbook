### Explicit absences / no specific cadence stated
- **Many items are risk-based or org-defined with no fixed interval given**:
  - “regular system reviews”
  - “regular basis”
  - “periodically”
  - “longer period of time”
  - “timely and efficient manner”
  - “as events occur and information changes”
- **Vulnerability scanning, penetration testing, audit-log review, and CDM** are mentioned, but **this page does not specify a fixed frequency** for those activities beyond the examples and general statements above.
