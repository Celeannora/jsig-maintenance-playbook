## Explicitly stated non-specific / risk-based cadence language

| Source section | Control family/control ID | Exact / closely paraphrased requirement | Cadence status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Security and privacy controls / control customization discussion | Various controls and enhancements | Controls may be tailored, and implementation may involve “organizationally defined parameters/values/attributes”; ARS customization must consider mission, business, risks, and environment. | **No specific cadence stated**; frequency may be **organization-defined** or **risk-based** |
| How can ARS be customized? | Various controls | “Business Owners must consider implementation of compensating controls as part of a **risk-based decision process**.” | **No specific cadence stated** |
| Security and privacy controls | Various controls | “Control Review Frequency” is the frequency in which the System Security Privacy Officers must review or evaluate the control; “Assessment Frequency” is the frequency in which the control must be assessed by a third-party assessor. | **No numeric cadence given in this section** |
| How can ARS be customized? / tailoring | Various controls | Tailoring can assign “specific values to organization-defined … control parameters” and may strengthen controls; reductions require documentation and AO approval. | **No specific cadence stated** |

## Absence notes
- The page does **not** provide additional daily, weekly, monthly, annual, 60-day, or 365-day operational maintenance schedules beyond the items listed above.
- The page does **not** provide specific cadence text for MA, CM, AU, SI, CA, CP, IR, IA controls on this page, except the PE-13 quarterly example and the AC-2 examples.
- The page does **not** list an explicit frequency for control review or assessment for all controls; it only defines those fields generally.
